Terms of Use

Swissplore ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and stored when you use the Swissplore mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Swissplore, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: Information required to create and manage your account, such as your email address and profile information.
  • User-submitted content: Information you choose to upload, including walking routes, descriptions, photos, and GPX files.
  • Location information: If you grant permission, Swissplore may use your device location while using walking and tracking features.
  • Subscription information: Information relating to your subscription status and access to paid features.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and improve Swissplore services.
  • Allow you to save, upload, and manage walking content.
  • Provide map, route, and tracking features.
  • Manage access to subscription features.
  • Communicate with you regarding your account or support requests.

3. User Uploaded Content

Swissplore allows users to submit walking routes, images, descriptions, and related content. Submitted content may be reviewed before being made available to other users.

You are responsible for ensuring that content you upload does not infringe the rights of others. Swissplore reserves the right to remove content that violates these terms or is considered inappropriate.

4. Location Data

Location permission is optional. If you enable location services, Swissplore may use your location to provide walking and tracking features.

Location data is used only to provide requested functionality and is not sold to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Swissplore uses third-party services to provide app functionality, including:

  • Supabase for database, authentication, and storage services.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play Store for subscription payments.
  • RevenueCat for subscription management and subscription status information.

Payment information is handled by Apple or Google. Swissplore does not store your payment card details.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

You can contact us at:

support.swissplore@proton.me

8. Children's Privacy

Swissplore is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate permission.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about Swissplore, using the app, or this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

support.swissplore@proton.me
